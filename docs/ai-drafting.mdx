# AI drafting

AI helps you start; you stay in charge. Every AI result in Qontent Tube is a **draft** that is validated, reviewable, and never overwrites your work.

## Draft a new video

Open **NEW** in the tools dock and describe the video:

- Topic or outline, formats (16:9, 9:16), roster, theme, length (~1, ~3, or ~5 minutes), and language.
- Qontent Tube drafts a script with scenes, speaker cues, and `[ANIM]` directions, then a storyboard for every scene.
- The draft is a **new** file marked as a draft. Existing files are never overwritten.

Review the script and storyboard, then choose **Accept draft**. Qontent Tube then asks whether to start voice generation now; nothing is generated unless you say so.

![NEW tool](/img/v020/new-tool.png)

## Storyboards from an existing script

In the Storyboard view, **Generate** creates elements for every scene from its dialogue and `[ANIM]` directions. **Regenerate scene** replaces one scene. Approved and locked content is kept.

## Connect a provider, or use manual mode

**Settings → AI assist** connects Anthropic Claude or OpenAI with your own key. Choose OpenAI and set an API base URL to use an OpenAI-compatible server such as Ollama. The key stays on your computer and is never shown in the browser again.

**Manual mode** needs no key: Qontent Tube shows the exact prompt, you paste it into any chat product, and paste the reply back. Replies go through the same checks.

## Model and effort

When you use a key, choose the model and the effort for each request:

| Effort | Typical wait | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fast** | 20–60 seconds | First drafts, variations, quick tries |
| **Balanced** | 1–2 minutes | More careful structure, fewer repairs |
| **Thorough** | 2–5 minutes | Long scripts and tricky refinements (more tokens) |

The note under the model explains what to expect: small models are fastest and cheapest, large models give the best quality at a higher cost. While a request runs you see the elapsed time and can **Cancel** at any time.

## Always valid

Replies must match the storyboard schema and the script: anchors in the right scene, known characters, unique IDs, the right speakers, meaningful scene titles, and the requested length. A reply that fails gets one automatic repair attempt; then you see exactly what was wrong.

## Provenance and privacy

Every AI run is recorded with the storyboard (provider, model, template version, and token usage) and shown under **AI provenance**. What is sent: your brief, or the selected scenes' titles, directions, and dialogue, plus the theme name and cast. Never sent: audio, other scripts, or your keys.
