# Make a product demo

This is the workflow we used for the [0.3 launch video](/blog/qontent-tube-0-3).

1. **Write the script.** One Markdown file, with one short idea per scene. Scene length follows the voice, or the word count until you have one.
2. **Record the product.** Capture only what each scene needs, with clean test data. A recording of a few seconds per scene is enough.
3. **Import** the files in [Assets](./capture-scenes#add-a-capture).
4. **Write headlines.** Give each scene a title and a one-line subtitle in Storyboard. Headlines carry the story, so the capture can stay small.
5. **Frame it.** Mark a region or two, then move the camera to what the line is about. Start wide, and return wide.
6. **Decide on AI, scene by scene.** Use the real capture when it shows the point. Use [an animation](./ai-adaptation) for ideas no screen shows.
7. **[Validate and approve](./validate-and-approve).** Fix what's listed, review privacy, approve.
8. **[Render](./rendering)**, then watch it through with sound.

## What you pay for

Real captures use no AI provider. Rendering runs on your computer with the Chrome and ffmpeg you already have. Costs come only from services you choose:

| Step | Without a provider | With a provider |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Captures, regions, camera | Included | Not needed |
| Rendering and re-rendering | Included | Not needed |
| Narration | Render silently with music | Your [voice service](./requirements) |
| AI scenes | Paste a reply from a chat product | Your AI key |

Generated results are stored, so once a scene is approved, later renders call no provider.

## Tips

- **Fix the recording, not the video.** If a screen shows the wrong thing, record it again.
- **Keep zoom modest.** Record at a higher resolution than your video if you want to zoom. Validation blocks visible upscaling.
- **Speed up before importing** if a take runs long. Qontent Tube plays recordings at their own speed.
