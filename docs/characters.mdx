# Characters

Characters make explainers feel alive. Qontent Tube ships the HAPI crew and lets you build your own. **Every character ships rigged**: idle breathing and blinking, waving, expressions (calm, happy, worried, curious), entrances and exits, and talking.

![Characters page with rig previews](/img/v020/characters.png)

## The collection

**Library → Characters** shows every character with its roles. The preview buttons play **Idle and blink**, **Talk**, **Wave**, **Happy**, and **Worried**.

**Upload image** adds a PNG, WebP, or SVG as an image character with a limited rig (bob, hop, tilt, entrances, and a bounce while talking). SVG files are cleaned of scripts and external links.

## Build a character

**New character** opens the builder:

1. Describe the character, pick a **style** (flat vector, soft 3D, line art, pixel) and a **personality** (curious, calm, hype, wise).
2. **Generate 4 variations**. Without an AI key they are procedural and reproducible from the seed shown; with AI assist the model picks parts and colors.
3. **Lock** the variations you like and **Regenerate unlocked**.
4. **Refine** one in words ("make the headset orange, add a scarf") and review the before/after and the list of changes before applying.
5. Name it, give it a role, choose who it plays, and **Add to collection**. Nothing is saved before that.

Characters are built from a curated parts library and drawn by Qontent Tube, so they always match the house style, can follow your theme, and never contain generated code.

## Cast and talk

The **CAST** tool lists the speakers of your script and the character each one plays, and tells you when someone is missing. In a storyboard, a character element without a specific character shows **whoever is speaking**.

When a clip is generated, Qontent Tube measures its loudness 30 times a second. The speaking character's mouth follows it and closes in the pauses; the crew's visors glow and bounce. The speaking character also steps forward slightly.
