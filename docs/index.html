Write the script. Hear the roster. Ship the video.

Qontent Tube is a Markdown-first voice and animation studio for Windows and Linux. It keeps your script, scenes, cast, narration, animation, and finished videos together in one calm workspace.

Start here​

  • Create your first voiceover — go from an empty workspace to a playable two-scene draft.
  • Write a structured script — learn the small Markdown grammar Qontent Tube understands.
  • Build a reusable cast — assign friendly voices and pacing to recurring speakers.
  • Generate and review narration — create only the audio you need and revise without rebuilding.
  • Work with local Markdown — bring your own editor and understand safe file reconciliation.
  • Desktop help — learn how the packaged app starts, stores data, and shuts down.

New in 0.2: it's alive​

  • Studio and the animated stage — preview every scene as an animated video that follows the voice.
  • Storyboards — edit scenes with forms, approve and lock them, and add AI backgrounds.
  • AI drafting — draft scripts and storyboards from a topic, with your key or by copy and paste.
  • Themes — bring your brand colors and fonts, with readability checks.
  • Characters — build a cast that talks with the real voice.
  • Languages — translate, re-voice, and keep every language in sync.
  • Mobile formats — adapt scenes for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok.
  • Rendering videos — export MP4, captions, chapters, and parts on your computer.

Qontent Tube is intentionally deterministic: it reads the structure you write instead of asking AI to guess where scenes and speakers begin. AI can draft for you, but every result is a reviewable draft, and your Markdown remains the canonical source.
