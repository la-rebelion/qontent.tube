# Write the script. Hear the roster. Ship the video.

Qontent Tube is a Markdown-first voice and animation studio for Windows and Linux. It keeps your script, scenes, cast, narration, animation, and finished videos together in one calm workspace.

## Start here

- **[Check requirements before buying](./requirements)** — see the supported systems, Deepgram key, render tools, optional AI services, and separate provider costs.
- **[Create your first voiceover](./getting-started)** — go from an empty workspace to a playable two-scene draft.
- **[Write a structured script](./writing-scripts)** — learn the small Markdown grammar Qontent Tube understands.
- **[Build a reusable cast](./rosters-and-voices)** — assign friendly voices and pacing to recurring speakers.
- **[Generate and review narration](./generation-and-playback)** — create only the audio you need and revise without rebuilding.
- **[Work with local Markdown](./files-and-storage)** — bring your own editor and understand safe file reconciliation.
- **[Desktop help](./desktop-and-troubleshooting)** — learn how the packaged app starts, stores data, and shuts down.

## New in 0.2: it's alive

- **[Studio and the animated stage](./studio-and-stage)** — preview every scene as an animated video that follows the voice.
- **[Storyboards](./storyboards)** — edit scenes with forms, approve and lock them, and add AI backgrounds.
- **[AI drafting](./ai-drafting)** — draft scripts and storyboards from a topic, with your key or by copy and paste.
- **[Themes](./themes)** — bring your brand colors and fonts, with readability checks.
- **[Characters](./characters)** — build a cast that talks with the real voice.
- **[Languages](./languages)** — translate, re-voice, and keep every language in sync.
- **[Mobile formats](./mobile-formats)** — adapt scenes for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok.
- **[Rendering videos](./rendering)** — export MP4, captions, chapters, and parts on your computer.

Qontent Tube is intentionally deterministic: it reads the structure you write instead of asking AI to guess where scenes and speakers begin. AI can draft for you, but every result is a reviewable draft, and your Markdown remains the canonical source.
