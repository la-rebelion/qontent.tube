# Studio and the animated stage

Qontent Tube 0.2 adds a stage: every script becomes an animated 1920 × 1080 video whose timing follows the voice. Studio is the start page. Pick a project and a script, then switch between three views that share your draft and the playhead.

| View               | What you see                                                                                        |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Script**         | The Markdown editor and the scene and speaker outline.                                              |
| **Script + Stage** | The editor beside the live stage, plus a timeline with scene, voice, animation, and caption tracks. |
| **Storyboard**     | Every scene with wireframes, its elements, and review controls. See [Storyboards](./storyboards).   |

![Script + Stage with the timeline](/img/v020/studio-stage.png)

## The stage follows the voice

Animation beats are anchored to dialogue lines, not to seconds. When a clip is regenerated or a sentence changes length, every card, chip, and character that belongs to that line moves with it. Until a line has voice, Qontent Tube estimates its length from the words, and the header shows the video length as _estimated_ or _voiced_.

- **Preview with estimated timing** plays the stage on its own clock before any audio exists.
- Click a scene, a voice block, or an animation beat on the timeline to jump there.
- The stage header switches between **16:9**, **9:16**, and **side by side** (see [Mobile formats](./mobile-formats)).
- The **Language** switch in the script header opens the same video in another language (see [Languages](./languages)).

## New script grammar

Two additions to the [script grammar](./writing-scripts):

- `[ANIM: …]` lines describe animation intent. They are never spoken; they appear on the timeline's animation track and guide AI drafting.
- Front matter keys `theme`, `formats` (`16:9`, `9:16`), and `languages` record production choices.

```markdown
---
roster: explainers
theme: hapi-blueprint
formats: [16:9, 9:16]
languages: [en, es-MX]
---

# Why Contracts Matter

## The Silent Break

[ANIM: hapi looks worried as error chips pile up]

**NARRATOR:**

One renamed field broke checkout.
```
