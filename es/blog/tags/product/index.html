Qontent Tube 0.2: it's alive

23 de octubre de 2026 · 5 min de lectura

In 0.1, Qontent Tube gave your script a voice. In 0.2, it gives it a stage.

Write the scenes. Hear the cast. Watch the cards slide in on the word that mentions them, the characters talk with the real voice, and the finished MP4 land in your folder — in English, in Spanish, and as Shorts.

The video below was made entirely with Qontent Tube 0.2. One Markdown script, one storyboard, two voices, a cast of two characters, three painted backgrounds, and three renders: this one, a Spanish version, and a vertical Short.

Introducing Qontent Tube

13 de octubre de 2026 · 3 min de lectura

Generating a voice is easy. Managing a complete voiceover is still surprisingly messy.

The script lives in one place, voice choices somewhere else, and every paragraph becomes another file to name and place. Rewrite one sentence and the whole production starts to feel uncertain again.

Qontent Tube keeps the script at the center of that work.
